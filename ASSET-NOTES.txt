These are AI-created illustrative photographs, not user photos or captured app screenshots. The website labels the lock-screen examples accordingly. Existing app screenshots are unmodified and shown in a separate labelled gallery. WebP encoding changes only the delivery format; clocks, notes, doodles and phone frames are HTML/CSS/SVG.

TWO-PHONE ANIMATED GIF
The live doodle illustration is exported as dist/images/live-doodle-two-phones.gif.
It is 800 x 850 pixels, loops over 12 seconds, and shows synchronised sequential
strokes on two different sample wallpapers. The illustration is code-rendered
from the existing SVG stroke artwork and generated photo assets, not an app capture.
The completed static frame is live-doodle-two-phones-still.webp.

Reproduce with the bundled sharp Node dependency available:
node capture/build-live-gif.cjs
python capture/encode-live-gif.py

Frame PNGs are ignored under capture/gif-frames/. GIF timing and strokes are in
capture/live-game-strokes.json. Palette-stable encoding keeps the photo backgrounds
steady and the delivered GIF small. The GIF can be downloaded from the website.
